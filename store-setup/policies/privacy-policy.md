This policy explains how **[Registered business name]** ("Homeward", "we", "us") collects and uses your personal information when you visit [your domain] or buy from us, and what your rights are under POPIA.

## 1. Who is responsible for your information

- **Business:** [Registered business name], registration number [CIPC registration number]
- **Address:** [physical address]
- **Information Officer:** [Information Officer name], [Information Officer email]

## 2. What we collect

- **Order and contact details:** name, email, phone number, delivery and billing address, and what you ordered.
- **Payment information:** card payments are processed by **Yoco**. We never receive or store your full card details.
- **Messages:** anything you send us by email, WhatsApp or our contact form.
- **Technical information:** IP address, browser and device type, and pages visited, collected through cookies (see section 7).
- **Marketing preferences:** whether you've agreed to receive emails from us.

We do **not** collect or have access to the location of your tracker cards. That information goes only to you, end-to-end encrypted, through Apple's Find My network.

## 3. Why we use it

- to process, deliver and support your order (contract);
- to answer your questions and handle returns and warranty claims (contract / legitimate interest);
- to prevent fraud and keep our store secure (legitimate interest);
- to keep accounting and tax records (legal obligation); and
- to send you marketing emails, **only if you opted in**. You can unsubscribe any time with the link in every email.

## 4. Who we share it with

We share only what's needed, with service providers ("operators") who process it on our behalf:

- **Shopify** (our e-commerce platform);
- **Yoco** (payment processing);
- **Aramex** (delivery: your name, address and phone number);
- [email provider, e.g. Shopify Email]; and
- [any review, analytics or advertising apps you install, e.g. Judge.me, Google, Meta].

We never sell your personal information.

## 5. Information sent outside South Africa

Some of our operators, including Shopify, store data on servers outside South Africa. We only use providers bound by data protection laws or agreements that give a level of protection substantially similar to POPIA, as section 72 requires.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep order records for at least **5 years** for tax and accounting purposes. Other information is kept only as long as it's needed for the purposes above, then deleted or anonymised.

## 7. Cookies

We use cookies that are needed for the store to work (for example to remember your cart) and, with your consent, analytics and marketing cookies. You can change your choice using the cookie banner or your browser settings.

## 8. Security

Our store runs over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, payments are handled by a PCI-DSS compliant provider, and access to customer information is limited to team members who need it.

## 9. Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

- ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
- ask us to correct or delete it;
- object to us processing it, including for direct marketing; and
- withdraw consent you've given.

To make a request, email [Information Officer email]. We'll respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you may complain to the **Information Regulator (South Africa)**: [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za).

## 10. Children

Our store isn't directed at children under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
