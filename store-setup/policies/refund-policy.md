At Homeward we want you to be happy with your purchase. This policy explains how returns, refunds and warranty claims work. It doesn't limit your rights under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (CPA) or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 (ECTA).

## 1. Changed your mind? 30-day returns

You may return your order within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund of the purchase price, as long as:

- the cards are unused and undamaged, with all original packaging and contents; and
- you have **removed the cards from your Apple Find My app** (Find My → Items → select the card → Remove Item). A card still linked to your Apple ID can't be checked or reused, and can't be refunded until it's removed.

Unless the item is faulty or we made a mistake, the cost of sending it back to us is for your account. Please use a tracked service (for example Aramex, PostNet or Pargo) and keep your proof of postage. We can't refund parcels that don't reach us.

## 2. Cooling-off period (ECTA section 44)

You may cancel any online purchase within **7 days of receiving the goods**, without giving a reason and without penalty. The only charge we may pass on is the direct cost of returning the goods. Our 30-day window already includes this period.

## 3. Faulty goods: 12-month warranty

Every card has a **12-month warranty** against manufacturing faults, from the date of delivery. If a card stops working through no fault of yours, we will, at your choice, **repair or replace it, or refund you**, and **we'll cover the courier costs both ways**.

This is in addition to your right under **section 56 of the CPA** to return defective goods within 6 months of delivery.

The warranty doesn't cover:

- damage from misuse, accidents, liquid or heat;
- normal wear and tear (for example scratches); or
- cards that have been opened, modified or repaired by someone else.

## 4. Damaged or incorrect items

If your parcel arrives damaged or contains the wrong item, please contact us as soon as you can (ideally within 48 hours) with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or refund you, and arrange the collection at our cost.

## 5. How to return an item

1. Contact us on WhatsApp ([WhatsApp number]) or email ([support email]) with your order number and the reason for the return.
2. Remove the cards from your Find My app.
3. We'll reply within one business day with our returns address and instructions. For warranty claims and damaged items, we'll book the Aramex collection for you.
4. Pack the item securely in its original packaging.

## 6. Refunds

Once your return reaches us and we've checked it, we'll refund the **full purchase price** to the card you paid with (via Yoco) within **5 working days**. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. Delivery was free, so there's no delivery fee to refund or deduct.

If a return doesn't meet the conditions above (for example the cards are damaged, or still linked to an Apple ID), we'll contact you to explain and, if you like, send it back to you.
