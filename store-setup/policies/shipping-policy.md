## Where we deliver

We deliver anywhere in **South Africa**, including outlying areas. We don't ship internationally at the moment.

## Delivery cost

Delivery is **free** on every order. There's no minimum spend and nothing extra is added at checkout.

## Courier

All orders are delivered **door-to-door by Aramex** with tracking.

## Dispatch times

- Orders placed **before 13:00 on a working day** (Monday to Friday, excluding South African public holidays) are dispatched **the same day**.
- Orders placed after 13:00, on weekends or on public holidays are dispatched **the next working day**.

## Delivery times

After dispatch, Aramex usually delivers in:

- **2–4 working days** to main centres (Johannesburg, Pretoria, Cape Town, Durban, Gqeberha, Bloemfontein and surrounds); and
- **3–6 working days** to outlying and rural areas.

These are estimates from Aramex and not guaranteed. Peak periods (for example Black Friday and December) and weather can cause delays.

## Tracking

As soon as your parcel is collected, we email you a shipping confirmation with your **Aramex tracking number**. You can also track it on our [Track your order](/pages/track-order) page.

## Your delivery address

Please give a full street address including complex or estate name, unit number and suburb, plus a cellphone number the driver can reach. A work address is a good idea if nobody is home during the day. We can't deliver to PO Boxes.

## Missed deliveries

If you're not available, Aramex will contact you to arrange another delivery or let you collect from your nearest Aramex branch.

## Lost or damaged parcels

If your parcel is lost or arrives damaged, contact us. We'll deal with Aramex and send a replacement or refund you. You don't have to chase the courier yourself. Ownership and risk pass to you once the parcel is delivered to the address you gave us.
