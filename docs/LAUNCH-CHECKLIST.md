# Launch checklist

Go through this before you take the password off. The theme is written to be honest. Each item below is a claim the store makes that has to be true for **your** business and **your** stock.

## 1. Confirm the product claims against your supplier

Ask your supplier for the spec sheet, or test a card yourself. Where the store's wording doesn't match, change it in **Customize** (or in the product description).

| Claim on the store | Where it appears | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Rechargeable battery with **wireless charging**, lasts **months** per charge | Product → Specifications; Product FAQ "How do I charge it?"; FAQ page → Battery & care | ☐ |
| Press the button to switch on; it **beeps** when ready | How it works (home + product); FAQ → Setting up | ☐ |
| Works with **iOS 14.5 or later** | Hero area, How it works footnote, FAQ, product page | ☐ |
| Supports **left-behind alerts** and **Lost Mode** | Home features, product description, FAQ | ☐ |
| Bluetooth range **10–30 m** | FAQ → "What's the range…" | ☐ |
| Box contains **2 cards + quick-start guide** (charger included?) | Product → What's in the box; product description | ☐ |
| Not designed to be submerged | FAQ → "Is it waterproof?" | ☐ |
| Card size **85 × 54 × 2 mm** | Several places (from the supplier photos) | ☐ |

**Apple branding:** the store says the card is *compatible with* Apple Find My, which is a fair description. Two of the supplier photos also show a "Works with … Apple Find My" style badge. Apple only allows its official badge on products certified through its MFi programme. Ask your supplier for proof of certification. If they can't provide it, consider asking for (or shooting) photos without the badge. The card itself is printed with the maker's name ("LoshallCard"), and the store copy never claims Homeward makes it.

## 3. Business details (ECT Act section 43)

- ☐ Registered name, registration number, physical address, email, phone in **Theme settings → Business details**
- ☐ VAT number added (if VAT-registered) and tax set to *include tax in prices*
- ☐ All five policies pasted in, with every `[placeholder]` replaced
- ☐ About and Contact pages show the "Who you're buying from" details correctly

## 4. Test like a customer (on your phone)

- ☐ Homepage → product → **Add to cart** → cart drawer opens → **Secure checkout**
- ☐ Checkout asks for a phone number, shows **Free Aramex delivery**, total **R599**
- ☐ Pay with **Yoco** (real card), receive the order confirmation email, then **refund** it
- ☐ Fulfil a test order with a real Aramex waybill and check the shipping email's tracking link works
- ☐ **Track your order** page opens the right Aramex result for a real waybill
- ☐ Contact form sends to your inbox; **WhatsApp** button opens a chat with your number
- ☐ All footer links work (FAQ, Shipping, Returns, Track, About, Contact, the four policies)
- ☐ Delivery countdown on the product page shows sensible dates

## 5. Look and feel

- ☐ Own logo uploaded (or happy with the built-in wordmark)
- ☐ Team section: add **real first names and photos** when you can. Real faces build trust. Leave the role-only cards until then rather than inventing people.
- ☐ "Note from us" sections: rewrite in your own words if you like, and sign with a real name
- ☐ Social links added (Theme settings → Social media), only for accounts that are active
- ☐ Branded email address set as the sender in Settings → Notifications

## 6. Housekeeping

- ☐ Cookie banner on (Settings → Customer privacy)
- ☐ Information Officer registered with the Information Regulator
- ☐ Google Search Console / Google & YouTube channel connected (optional)
- ☐ Password removed: Online Store → Preferences
