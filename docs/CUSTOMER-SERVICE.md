# Customer service playbook

The store promises *real people, quick replies*. This keeps everyone on the team answering the same way. Save these as **WhatsApp Business quick replies** (type `/` to use them) and as email templates.

## Daily routine

**Every working day before 13:00**

1. Shopify → Orders → **Unfulfilled**.
2. Check each order: address complete (complex/unit, suburb), phone number present.
3. Pack: 2 cards + quick-start guide in a padded mailer.
4. Book the Aramex waybill → fulfil in Shopify with the tracking number (the customer is emailed automatically).
5. Hand parcels to Aramex / have them collected.

**Morning and afternoon**: clear WhatsApp, email and the contact-form inbox. Aim to reply within a few hours. The store promises within one business day.

**Weekly**: check Aramex tracking for any parcel older than 4 working days and contact the customer before they contact you.

## Quick replies

### /hello: first response
> Hi [name], thanks for getting in touch with Homeward! [Your name] here. How can I help?

### /android: Android question
> Hi [name]! Unfortunately the card only works with Apple's Find My app, so it needs an iPhone or iPad (iOS 14.5 or later). It can't be used with Android phones. Sorry about that! Let me know if there's anything else I can help with.

### /where: where is my order?
> Hi [name], thanks for your patience! Your order [#1234] left us on [day] with Aramex. Your tracking number is [waybill]. You can follow it here: [tracking link]. Main centres usually take 2–4 working days. If it hasn't moved in 2 working days, let me know and I'll chase Aramex for you.

### /notshipped: order not yet dispatched
> Hi [name], your order [#1234] is packed and goes out with Aramex [today/tomorrow]. You'll get an email with the tracking number as soon as it's collected.

### /setup: setup help
> Happy to help! Here's how to add it:
>
> 1. Press the button on the card. It beeps when it's ready.
> 2. On your iPhone open **Find My** → **Items** → **+** → **Add Other Item**.
> 3. Hold the card right next to your phone and follow the steps.
>
> If it doesn't show up: check Bluetooth is on and the card isn't already linked to another Apple ID. Still stuck? Send me a screenshot and we'll sort it out.

### /return: change-of-mind return
> No problem, [name]. You're within our 30-day return window. Please:
>
> 1. Remove the cards from Find My (Find My → Items → tap the card → Remove Item).
> 2. Pack them in the original packaging.
> 3. Send them with a tracked service to: [returns address]. Reference: [#1234].
>
> Send me the tracking number, and we'll refund R599 to your card within 5 working days of receiving it.

### /faulty: warranty claim
> Sorry to hear that, [name]! That's covered by our 12-month warranty. Could you tell me what's happening (or send a short video)? Please remove the faulty card from Find My, and I'll book an Aramex collection at our cost. We'll send a replacement as soon as it's on its way, or refund you if you'd prefer.

### /damaged: arrived damaged
> I'm really sorry about that, [name]. Could you send a photo of the parcel and the card? We'll send a replacement straight away and arrange collection of the damaged one at no cost to you.

### /payment: payment trouble
> Our checkout uses Yoco for card payments. If your card was declined, it's usually the bank's online-payment security. Try approving it in your banking app, or use another card. If you were charged but didn't get an order confirmation, send me the time and amount and I'll check right away.

### /review: ask for a review (about 7 days after delivery)
> Hi [name], hope the cards are settling in nicely! If you have a minute, an honest review would mean a lot to a small South African team like ours: [review link]. Thanks so much! [Your name], Homeward

## Tone

- Use the customer's name, and sign off with your own first name.
- Keep it short, warm and specific. Give dates, tracking numbers and next steps.
- If we got something wrong, say so plainly and fix it. Don't argue over a R599 order. Keep the customer.
