How delivery works

Your CLIMORA order will be delivered through our courier partner, Aramex. Estimated delivery is 5–10 business days, subject to order processing and courier collection.

Delivery costs

Delivery costs are calculated at checkout based on your address, and are always shown before you pay.

Order confirmation and tracking

You’ll receive an order confirmation email when you place your order, and a shipping confirmation email once your parcel has been handed to Aramex. When tracking is available for your shipment, the tracking details are included in that email.

Delivery address

Please make sure your delivery address and phone number are complete and correct, as the courier may need to contact you. Contact us as soon as possible if you need to change your address after ordering.

Delays

Delivery times are estimates. Occasionally, delays may occur due to high volumes, public holidays, weather or other circumstances outside our control. If your order is taking longer than expected, please contact us and we’ll help.
