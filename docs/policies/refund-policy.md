## Cooling-off period (changed your mind)
In terms of section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, 2002 (ECTA), you may cancel an online purchase without reason or penalty within **7 days of receiving the goods**.
- Items must be returned unused, in their original condition and packaging, with all accessories.
- The only charge you are responsible for is the **direct cost of returning the goods** to us.
- We will refund payments received within 30 days of the cancellation.

## Defective goods
In terms of section 56 of the Consumer Protection Act, 2008 (CPA), if a product is defective or does not meet the standard you are entitled to expect, you may return it within **6 months of delivery**, and choose a **repair, replacement or refund**. This applies unless the product was misused, not used according to its instructions, or damaged after delivery. We may need to assess the product first.
